# Safety Disclaimer

PK Tracker is a quantified-self visualization tool. It is provided for educational,
informational, and personal tracking purposes only.

PK Tracker is **not** a medical device, diagnostic tool, clinical decision-support
system, or substitute for professional medical advice. The software does not
recommend, prescribe, adjust, or validate any dose of any medication or substance.
Prescription medication decisions must be made only with a qualified medical
professional.

All concentration, effect, clearance, peak-time, sleep-cutoff, and alcohol/BAC
values are estimates derived from simplified population-average models. Real
pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics vary substantially between individuals due
to body mass, sex, age, genetics, liver function, tolerance, food intake, sleep,
other substances, medications, illness, and many other factors.

Alcohol-related outputs are rough estimates only. They must **never** be used to
decide whether it is safe or legal to drive, operate machinery, perform hazardous
work, or make any safety-critical decision.
